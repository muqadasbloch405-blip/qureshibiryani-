Fifty years, one recipe

Qureshi Biryani has been cooking at this Pakwan Center on Ketchury Road since 1975 — long enough that grandfathers now bring their grandchildren for the same plate they grew up on.

Every deg is built the old way: whole spices bought fresh, meat simmered until it falls off the bone, and rice finished on dum until the two are inseparable. Nothing shortcut, nothing changed.
